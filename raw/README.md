during the iterative process of preparing applications for the 2026

- `Xuzhou Wang 2.pdf` — analytics / product-analyst voice
- `Xuzhou Wang 3.pdf` — engineering / data-engineering voice
- `Xuzhou Wang Rseume 4.pdf` — extended version including CITIC Futures and CICC, plus academic projects
- `Xuzhou Wang Rseume5.pdf` — strategy / consulting voice (Kyraton Home foregrounded)
- `Xuzhou Wang Rseume 6.pdf` — analyst / data-pipeline voice

across all six versions.
