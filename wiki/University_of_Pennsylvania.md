This article documents [[Colar Wang]]'s graduate study at the
**University of Pennsylvania** (UPenn), which he entered in August 2025
as a candidate for the Master of Science in Systems Engineering.[^1]

## Program

The Master of Science in Systems Engineering is offered by UPenn's
Department of Electrical and Systems Engineering, within the School of
Engineering and Applied Science. The program sits at the intersection
of applied mathematics, machine learning, and systems modeling — a
combination Wang has described as *"engineering grammar for people who
build AI products."*

## Coursework

Wang's coursework, drawn from the systems-engineering and data-science
tracks, has emphasized:

- **Applied Machine Learning**
- **Statistics for Data Science**
- **Simulation Modeling**
- **Marketing Analytics**

## Context within the broader biography

Wang's move to Philadelphia in August 2025 immediately preceded the
founding of [[KitchenSurvivor]] in November 2025, and coincided with
the conception of [[AgentConfig]] in early 2026. Both products draw
methodologically on the quantitative and modeling foundations of the
program, and Wang has cited the simulation-modeling and applied-ML
coursework as directly relevant to the
[[KitchenSurvivor|edge–cloud inference pipeline]] he designed.
